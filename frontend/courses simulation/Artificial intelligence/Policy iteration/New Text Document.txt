            <h2 class="text-xl font-bold text-indigo-400">Theory: Policy Iteration</h2>
            <p class="text-sm text-slate-300">Policy Iteration is an iterative algorithm for finding the optimal policy for a Markov Decision Process (MDP). It alternates between two steps: <strong>Policy Evaluation</strong> (calculating state values for the current policy) and <strong>Policy Improvement</strong> (updating the policy to act greedily with respect to current values).</p>
        </div>

        <!-- Procedure Tab -->
        <div id="content-procedure" class="tab-content hidden bg-slate-800 border border-slate-700 rounded-xl p-6 space-y-4">
            <h2 class="text-xl font-bold text-indigo-400">Procedure</h2>
            <ol class="list-decimal list-inside space-y-2 text-sm text-slate-300">
                <li>Initialize an arbitrary policy $\pi(s)$.</li>
                <li><strong>Evaluation:</strong> Solve the Bellman equation for current policy to get values $V(s)$.</li>
                <li><strong>Improvement:</strong> For each state, update $\pi(s)$ to select the action that maximizes expected utility.</li>
                <li>Repeat until $\pi(s)$ stops changing.</li>
            </ol>
        </div>
